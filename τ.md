# τ — the progressive trit field

A cascade of base-100 pairs. One pair per level of refinement.

    depth 1    "37"           a valid field on its own
    depth 2    "37 12"        refines it — the first pair is unchanged
    depth 3    "37 12 05"     refines again

## Why base 100

One level of an address is one R4 element on each of three faces:

    3 faces × 4 states = 64 combinations       fits 00..99, with 36 spare
    6 bits per level per cell

And the base is not chosen. It is what the beat and the bleed multiply to:

    Φ⁷ × 100φ⁷  =  29.0344 × 3.4442  =  100.000000

The same identity from the ledger's own geometry, now setting the width of a digit.

There is now a second derivation of the same exact beat/bleed pair from the six-field
null construction: `z = 010`, `V = 050`, `6V = 3`, tenfold closure gives `30`, the
directed seam step `t = -01` gives `29`, and reciprocal scale inversion through the
null gives

    ε = 1 / (29 + ε)
    ε² + 29ε - 1 = 0
    ε = φ⁷                 29 + ε = Φ⁷

The derivation and the boundary between exact algebra and proposed field rules are
recorded in [`math/SIX-FIELD-DERIVATION.md`](math/SIX-FIELD-DERIVATION.md).

## Why it is progressive

Addresses are a prefix code — verified, `prefix property holds: true`. A coarse render
is a **strict truncation** of a fine one, so refinement never re-decodes and never
discards. Send 6 bits, you have a field. Send 6 more, it sharpens. Stop anywhere.

## What a single face is

Each face is exactly **4-adic**. Distance is `4^(-k)` where k is the depth of first
divergence, and that is an ultrametric — tested on all 818,805 triples of live-byte
addresses:

    face 0 (P) alone        strong triangle inequality 100.00%   isosceles 100.00%
    face 1 (Z) alone        100.00%   100.00%
    face 2 (N) alone        100.00%   100.00%
    max over three faces    100.00%   100.00%
    sum over three faces     44.01%

Zero violations. The branching factor is 4 = |R4| — the Klein group's branching *is*
the p-adic structure. The address is not p-adic-like; it is p-adic.

The **sum** is what escapes it. In an ultrametric there is no betweenness and no
interpolation; a max over faces keeps that, a sum does not. Three ultrametrics summed
is Archimedean, which is why three faces separate what one cannot — with a single face,
`7` and `9` had identical addresses.

Two scales run at once: byte identity saturates at 18 bits (3 levels), while position
in the field keeps refining to depth 12. A stream can resolve *what* almost immediately
and keep resolving *where* indefinitely.

## Rendering

`index.html` renders it live behind the page — the loaded ledger's trits where the
sunflower puts them, drawn only where the field is working. `τ.render()` and `τ.png()`
make a still of the same thing for reading outside a browser: Vogel placement at 2πkφ,
three layers offset by φ⁷. The parastichy arms appear without being drawn — they are
what 2πkφ does when you let it run.

## What the live render shows

Three faces of one trit sit at `2πLφ⁷`, so within any trit they are **12.399°** apart —
the same fan, everywhere in the field. Consecutive trits turn by the golden angle,
**222.492°**. The two differ by exactly

    2πφ / 2πφ⁷  =  φ⁻⁶  =  Φ⁶  =  17.944272

one rung below the beat at `Φ⁷ = 29.034442`. So the flower is a single figure repeated
at golden-angle steps, which is why the triads read as oriented to one another: they
are all oriented identically to their own radius.
